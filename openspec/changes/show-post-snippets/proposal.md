## Why

The Sentiment panel showed counts and long explanatory text but no example posts. The owner asked for less text and a few post samples with their tone.

## What changes

- The Sentiment panel is trimmed to a sample select, tone count buttons, one meta line, a post list and a Bluesky search link. Model and limit notes move into a collapsed "About this model" section.
- The count buttons filter the list by tone (Positive, Neutral, Negative) and carry thumb icons.
- The top and latest samples return up to 5 post snippets per tone, with tone label, possible-irony flag, time and link.
- No author handle, name, DID text or avatar is returned or shown.
- Mock and public deployments, and the live stream sample, return no post text.
